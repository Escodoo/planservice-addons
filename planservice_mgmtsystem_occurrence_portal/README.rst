Expose occurrence records to the supplier on the customer portal so the
partner can fill the response, attach evidence and submit the record for
inspector verification.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Configuration
=============

Grant the supplier contact portal access and set that company (or a
child contact) as the occurrence partner. Releasing the record to the
supplier generates a portal token and notifies the partner.

Usage
=====

Portal users whose commercial partner matches the occurrence partner see
**Occurrences** on ``/my``.

They can open a released record, update the supplier section, add
treatment actions and evidence, then submit the response. Inspector
fields stay read-only.
