Enter timesheet lines as a percentage of the employee's working day. The
hours are calculated from the resource calendar (or the company
calendar) and stored on the standard Time Spent field.

The sum of all timesheets of an employee on a given date cannot exceed
100% of that working day, regardless of project or task.

Usage
=====

1. Open a timesheet list or the **Timesheets** tab on a task.
2. Set **Day %** (for example 50). **Time Spent** is filled from the
   employee's average hours per day (50% of 8 h = 4 h).
3. Editing Time Spent updates Day % the other way around.
4. Saving a line that would take the employee over 100% for that date is
   refused, even if the other lines are on different projects or tasks.
