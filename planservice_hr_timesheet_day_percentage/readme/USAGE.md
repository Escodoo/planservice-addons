1. Open a timesheet list or the **Timesheets** tab on a task.
2. Set **Day %** (for example 50). **Time Spent** is filled from the employee's average
   hours per day (50% of 8 h = 4 h).
3. Editing Time Spent updates Day % the other way around.
4. Saving a line that would take the employee over 100% for that date is refused, even
   if the other lines are on different projects or tasks.
